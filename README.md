# Awesome-Environmental-Compliance-Management

# Top Environmental Compliance Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Environmental Permits, Emissions, Waste, Audits, Regulatory Tracking, Corrective Actions & EHS Compliance*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Environmental Compliance Management**. These systems help organizations track environmental permits, emissions, waste, spills, audits, regulatory obligations, corrective actions, and overall environmental performance within broader EHS or ESG programs.

**Examples** include Enablon, Sphera, Intelex, Cority, VelocityEHS, EcoOnline, ProcessMAP, Benchmark ESG, IsoMetrix, and Quentic (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Full environmental compliance and multi-site EHS platforms are almost entirely commercial. Practical open options include general checklist/audit engines, document control, incident tracking, and related environmental data tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Enablon (Wolters Kluwer)](https://www.enablon.com/)**  
  Enterprise EHS and sustainability platform with strong environmental compliance, permit, audit, and performance management capabilities.

- **[Sphera](https://sphera.com/)**  
  Operational risk and ESG platform covering environmental compliance, product stewardship, and sustainability data management.

- **[Intelex](https://www.intelex.com/)**  
  Cloud EHSQ platform with environmental modules for permits, emissions, waste, audits, incidents, and regulatory compliance.

- **[Cority](https://www.cority.com/)**  
  Enterprise EHS software supporting environmental compliance, industrial hygiene, audits, and operational risk management.

- **[VelocityEHS](https://www.velocityehs.com/)**  
  EHS platform focused on chemical management, environmental compliance, safety, and related operational controls.

- **[EcoOnline](https://www.ecoonline.com/)**  
  EHS and chemical management platform with environmental compliance, risk assessment, and workplace safety features.

- **[ProcessMAP](https://www.processmap.com/)**  
  Integrated EHS and sustainability platform used for environmental compliance, audits, and enterprise risk workflows.

- **[Benchmark ESG / Benchmark Gensuite](https://www.benchmarkesg.com/)**  
  ESG and EHS compliance platform supporting environmental performance, audits, and multi-site program management.

- **[IsoMetrix](https://www.isometrix.com/)**  
  Integrated risk and compliance platform with environmental, health, safety, and sustainability modules.

- **[Quentic and related EHS/ESG platforms](https://www.quentic.com/)**  
  European EHS and environmental management software for compliance, audits, and operational risk.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Combining open checklists, incident trackers, and document control for smaller single-site or low-complexity programs.
- Using open GIS and data pipelines to support monitoring and reporting workflows.
- Accepting that multi-site permit management, automated regulatory change tracking, enterprise audit programs, chemical/product stewardship, and certified compliance evidence packs still favor commercial platforms (Enablon, Sphera, Intelex, Cority, VelocityEHS, EcoOnline, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, transparency, and lower cost for organizations with limited scope or strong internal IT.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Maintain permit and obligation registers → capture inspections and monitoring via open forms/sensors → log incidents and corrective actions → store evidence with version control → produce basic compliance reports. Suitable for small operators and internal tool teams. Most multi-site and regulated industrial organizations rely on commercial environmental compliance / EHS platforms for consistency and audit defensibility.
